About Corporate & Industrial

Narasinema Indonesia produces corporate and industrial video content designed to communicate information clearly and professionally. We help organizations document activities, explain processes, and share internal or external messages through visual storytelling.

Projects may include safety induction videos, corporate communication, internal communication, travel videos, and activity videos. We align the production approach with your location, access requirements, safety procedures, and communication objectives.

Clear Communication for Real Work

We plan each production with attention to the message, location, people involved, and practical requirements of the workplace.

A well-planned corporate video makes important information easier to understand and share.

Safety Induction

Present workplace procedures, safety expectations, and essential site information in a structured visual format.

Corporate & Internal Communication

Help teams and stakeholders understand company initiatives, messages, and updates.

Travel & Activity Video

Document business trips, company programs, operational activities, and organizational milestones.

Production Planning

Coordinate filming schedules, locations, permissions, and required stakeholders before the shoot.
